Music Hall — Florida Cinema of Banda Primitiva

2019

Architectural proposal for the Florida Cinema of Banda Primitiva in Paiporta, designed as a chamber-music hall and rehearsal complex addressing both acoustic and light performance.

Academic Project Workshop on the Acoustic Design of Auditoriums
Supervisor Prof. Ignacio Enrique Guillén Guillamón (Universitat Politècnica de València)

Individual Project
Sep 2019 – Dec 2019

Location Paiporta, Valencia, Spain

Tools UPV acoustic software (similar to CATT-Acoustic), AutoCAD, Sketchup, Adobe Ps/Ai/Id, Excel

How can architectural form translate acoustic precision into spatial emotion? How can light and sound together define the experience of a performance hall? Sound travels as light does: reflected, diffused, and gently held by form.
