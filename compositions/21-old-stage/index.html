Old Stage for New Life

2022

Urban regeneration and heritage enhancement in the historic center of Meda, reactivating abandoned ruins into cultural and public spaces. The site, surrounded by abandoned courtyards, becomes a stage for civic activity, a void that invites participation rather than a plaza to be filled.

Design Studio Research Thematic Studio
Supervisors Prof. Laura Anna Pezzetti, Prof. Rajendra Singh Adhikari, Prof. Giuliana Cardani (Politecnico di Milano)

Group Project
2-person collaboration with Yichuan Guo
Sep 2022 – Feb 2023

Location Città di Meda, Lombardy, Italy
Urban Analysis: ~150 ha
Architecture Design: 1,860 m²

Tools AutoCAD, Rhino, Enscape, Adobe Ps/Ai/Id

The “stage” acts as both metaphor and method: a space where the everyday and the historical meet, turning the void of absence into a framework for new forms of life.
