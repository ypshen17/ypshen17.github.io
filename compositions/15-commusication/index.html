Musical complex and design guidelines for the revitalization of a declining urban hotspot, an elementary exploration of Sonic Urbanism crafts

Degree Project B.Arch Thesis
Supervisor Prof. Wenyi Zhu (Tsinghua University)

Individual Project
Feb 2021 - Jun 2021

Location West Gate-Grand Cross, Xining, China
Urban analysis: 27.6 ha
Architecture design: 27,000 m²

Tools Rhino+VisualARQ (BIM), Adobe Ps/Ai/Id/Pr/Au

Inspired by church bells, mosque calls to prayer, and temple chanting, the design experiments with sonic hybridity resonate with Xining’s historical soundscape while remaining resilient to environmental and urban change.
