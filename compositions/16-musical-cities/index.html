A Study on Sara Adhitya’s Musical Cities

2022

Academic review introducing Dr. Sara Adhitya’s design research on music and the city into Chinese urban studies, summarising her work Musical Cities: Listening to Urban Design and Planning (UCL Press, 2017) through three interrelated approaches:
Sonified Urban Masterplan, Composing the City, and Urban Sonic Interaction Design, corresponding to the processes of listening, composing, and performing within urban design.

Academic Article Urban Design (城市设计) Vol. 2022 No. 1 pp. 44–57
Originally published in Chinese, translated by the author

Authors Yipeng Shen, Wenyi Zhu (Tsinghua University)
Jan 2022

Location Global case studies

Method Academic writing, Critical review, Theoretical analysis (Adobe Id)

Listening, composing, and performing form the three movements of a musical city.
