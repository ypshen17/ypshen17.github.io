Green Valley in Marrakech

2022

Sensory · Cultural Exchange in Scent · Architecture · Urban Design · Landscape · History · GIS

The design proposes a linear green corridor connecting the Medina, Gueliz, and Agdal districts, using vegetation and architecture to respond to Marrakech’s dry wind and layered urban history, forms a recognizable pattern seen from airplanes landing at Menara Airport.
Three public within the revitalization proposal buildings anchor the valley and link tourism, local livelihoods, and everyday life. The Spice Center integrates traditional souk patterns with flexible cooking and market units.

Design Studio M.Arch Design Studio 2
Honorable Mention AIAC 2022
Supervisors Prof. Xiaoqing Chen, Prof. Huan Zou (Tsinghua University)

Group Project
Yuchun Zhang, Yipeng Shen, Yuhan Wang
Feb 2022 – Sep 2022

Location Marrakech, Morocco
Urban Analysis: 105 ha
Architecture Design: 3,400 m²

Tools Rhino, Enscape, AutoCAD, Adobe Ps/Ai/Id

Landscape become a language of encounter, the green valley becomes a space of exchange:
between tradition and modernity,
between earth and air,
between what is seen from above and lived from within.
