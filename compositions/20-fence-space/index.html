On|Off the Fence Space

2023

subtitle: IC Urban Ethnography Report, 2023 Ethnographic study of marginal fence-lined spaces in Milan.
Explores appropriation, informality, and affordances. Scored 30/30. location: Milan, Italy tools: Ethnography, AutoCAD, NVivo gallery_path: assets/compositions/fence-space

Observation-based study along Naviglio Martesana, Milan, investigating how fences act as linear urban infrastructures that regulate and invite human and non-human activities.

Research Project (Urban Ethnography) Supervisor: Prof. Laura Anna Pezzetti
Individual Project
Date: May – July 2023

Location: Naviglio Martesana, Milan
Method: Field observation · photographic documentation · spatial mapping
Tools: Camera · InDesign · Illustrator
