On|Off the Fence Space

2023

Observation-based study along Naviglio Martesana, Milan, investigating how fences act as linear urban infrastructures that regulate and invite human and non-human activities.

Research Project IC Urban Ethnography
Supervisors Prof. Paola Briata, Prof. Massimo Bricocoli (Politecnico di Milano)

Individual Project
Sep 2022 – Feb 2023

Location Naviglio Martesana, Milan, Italy

Method Field observation, photographic documentation, spatial mapping
Tools Camera, Adobe Ps/Ai/Id

Breaking or setting up the rule to get on or off the fence space
often means entering dialogue with water and life.
