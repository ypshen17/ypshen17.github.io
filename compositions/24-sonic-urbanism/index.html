A Sonic Urbanism Passeggiata

2025

Multiscale analysis and conceptual design based on Sonic Urbanism theories along Milan’s South-East Axis: a Listening – Scoring – Performing process

Degree Project (M.Arch/MSc Thesis)
Supervisors: Prof. Laura Anna Pezzetti (Politecnico di Milano),
Prof. Wenyi Zhu (Tsinghua University)

Individual Project
Apr 2023 – Apr 2025

Location: Milan, Italy
Urban analysis: 5.8 km arterial axis
Architecture design: 6 nodes

Tools: GIS, Rhino+Grasshopper, Adobe Ps/Ai/Id/Pr, Sonic Pi

How to understand and represent the qualities of urban space through the discipline of sound? How to design urban spaces from the perspective of sound to create a more musical way of life?
