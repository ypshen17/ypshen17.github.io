/SLASH/

2021

Multifunctional complex for the revitalization of a transportation junction in 2035, Lishui Bridge, Beijing, envisioning new forms of urban life between motion and rest.

Studio M.Arch Design Studio 1, Prof. Zhu Wenyi
Team Yipeng Shen, Luo Hualong
Sep 2021 – Dec 2021

Urban Analysis 104 ha
Architecture Design 51,000 m²

The project rethinks infrastructure as both a connector and a retreat. It anticipates autonomous driving and mixed pedestrian systems while addressing the social fatigue of post-industrial Beijing.

Above, a slash platform hosts co-working studios, cafés, and open decks for the “multi-hyphenated” generation.
Below, quiet gardens and shaded terraces form a Lying-Flat Paradise — a civic space for stillness and recovery.

Tools AutoCAD, Rhino, Lumion, Adobe Ps/Ai/Id

How can infrastructure accept stillness?
How might doing nothing become a new form of participation?
