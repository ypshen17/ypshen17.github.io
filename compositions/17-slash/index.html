2021

Multifunctional complex for the revitalization of a transportation junction in 2035, envisioning new forms of urban life between motion and rest, rethinks infrastructure as both a connector and a retreat.

Design Studio M.Arch Design Studio 1
Supervisor Prof. Wenyi Zhu (Tsinghua University)

Group Project Yipeng Shen, Hualong Luo
Sep 2021 – Dec 2021

Location Lishui Bridge, Beijing, China
Urban Analysis: 104 ha
Architecture Design: 51,000 m²

Tools Rhino, Lumion, AutoCAD, Adobe Ps/Ai/Id

It anticipates autonomous driving and mixed pedestrian systems while addressing the social fatigue of post-industrial Beijing.
How can infrastructure accept stillness? How might doing nothing become a new form of participation?
