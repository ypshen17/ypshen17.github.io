Yipeng Shen's Self-identity System

2025

This composition develops my personal visual identity as both an architectural researcher and a multidisciplinary creator. The emblem condenses identity into a single sign, while its animation and website integration extend it into a living system. It demonstrates how self-representation can itself become a composition — balancing visual clarity, symbolic resonance, and cross-media adaptability.

The emblem is built from a flowing “S” curve, referencing both the initial of my surname and the architectural qualities of proportion and rhythm. Its minimal geometry and monochrome palette emphasize clarity and recognizability, while allowing flexible interpretation across academic and artistic contexts.

Sketching: Initial drafts hand-drawn in Procreate to explore curvature and proportion. Refinement: Vector lines finalized in Illustrator, adjusting the balance and flow. Animation: A short video created with Illustrator and After Effects, adding transitions of material and light to test how the emblem performs in motion. Website Integration: The emblem anchors my personal website, functioning both as a logo and a curatorial identity for academic and creative works.

Designing a personal emblem means turning the designer’s own identity into the subject of design. I treated “myself” as a composition — applying the same methods of iteration, abstraction, and testing that I use in urban and architectural compositions. The work shows how self-representation is not only practical branding but also critical design research: a negotiation between personal meaning and public readability.
