THROUGH

2019

Sensory · Light as a Way to Speak in Silence · Cultural Exchange
Architecture · Landscape · History · Poetry

Abstract painting pavilion for the intervention of a ruined meditative site. Designed as a meditation chapel and exhibition space for works by Manuel Rey Fueyo, the project explores how light, shadow, and material evoke reflection and silence.

Design Studio 4 Spaces for art exhibition and contemplation
Supervisor Prof. Pascual Sellés Cantos (Universitat Politècnica de València)

Individual Project
with group site survey Sep 2019 – Dec 2019

Location Desierto de Las Palmas, Benicàssim, Castelló, Spain
Landscape design: 4,150 m²
Architecture design: 920 m²

Tools Sketching, AutoCAD, SketchUp, Adobe Ps/Ai, physical model

How can we walk through light and shadow as memory itself? How can architecture, time, and silence overlap in one act of contemplation?
