Reflections

  • Blue hour commuters shaped the deck choreography more gently than expected; repetition smoothed interviews.
  • Market mezzanine shadows mapped a staff-only corridor worth returning with thermal camera.

Field Note

Sailed into the city watching light catch steel cables and commuter shoes, keeping one track in the recorder for every horn blast.

Observation Highlights

  • Step-free ferry route kept pace with stroller traffic without needing staff support.
  • The market mezzanine became a balcony for scent and sound, not a retail floor.
  • Evening harbor breeze tuned the violinist’s dynamics, giving the day a closing cadence.

Next Iteration

Return on a Sunday to log family rhythms, and bring a thermocouple to compare canopy temperature swings.

Limits

  • Dawn ferry observations rely on fair-weather sampling; typhoon days remain undocumented.
  • Sound notes are qualitative distillations; comfort rubric excludes humidity for now.
