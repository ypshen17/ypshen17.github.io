I spent the evening tracing invisible stems.

Each drag left a trail of petals that breathed, faded, and reappeared,
as if the screen remembered every motion.
It was my first brushset in p5.js,
a Field of Forms growing from code and chance.

The shapes oscillated between control and accident.
They carried the rhythm of my hand, the echo of my own hesitation.
Every color lingered, every sound a delayed pulse,
a tiny act of composition between the mechanical and the organic.

What began as debugging a trail became a quiet garden of gestures.
Not perfect, but alive enough to hum when silence fell.

Maybe that’s all I wanted:
to let code remember what touch forgets.
