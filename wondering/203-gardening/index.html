Balcony gardening began as a small experiment,
an echo of my grandparents long ago.
It started with five sunflower seeds,
a pot on the window ledge leaning toward the sun.

Soon the space became a quiet companion,
teaching patience through soil and leaves.

Each sprout is a reminder
that growth can happen in miniature,
and that even a narrow ledge
can open into a garden.
