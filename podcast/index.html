🎧 Thesis Podcast - Sonic Urbanism

Selected readings from my master's thesis documented as an album-length podcast. Each episode walks through the Listening - Scoring - Performing framework developed along Milan's south-east axis, weaving research, field recordings, and narrative reflections.

SoundCloud Playlist Vimeo Playlist

Episode 01

Introduction

Framing the questions and sonic methods.

An overview of Sonic Urbanism as a design practice, outlining the Milan corridor, guiding research questions, and why listening becomes the first act of design.

SoundCloud Vimeo

Episode 02

Framework

Listening - Scoring - Performing.

A deep dive into the analytical framework, including field recording strategies, sonic mapping, and the translation of waveforms into architectural notation.

SoundCloud Vimeo

Episode 03

Routes

Walking the South-East Axis.

Two soundwalk itineraries connect the Navigli district to the agricultural fringe, revealing how rhythm, tempo, and resonance shift through public space.

SoundCloud Vimeo

Episode 04

Performances

Prototyping sonic public space.

The concluding set of episodes translates sonic notation into spatial interventions, highlighting seven strategies for designing public space as an instrument.
