Urban Designer · Space Researcher · Sonic Urbanism Storyteller

I am a designer and researcher working at the intersections of architecture, urbanism, and sound. My work explores multisensory approaches to public space, with listening, scoring, and performing as critical methods for reimagining the city.

Having recently graduated with double Master's degrees in Architecture and Urban Design from Politecnico di Milano and Tsinghua University, I am now preparing for new academic and research opportunities, including doctoral studies, post-graduate research, or collaborative design projects. My interests extend to critical urban theory, participatory design, and interdisciplinary practices that connect the city with sound, art, and everyday life.

This site functions as both portfolio and archive: a place to share my academic research, design experiments, and artistic explorations within and beyond sonic urbanism.
